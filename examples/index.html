web-demos

Three vanilla JS components. No framework, no build step. Open these files directly from disk and they work.

Demos

  • modal-demo.html — dialog with focus trap
  • tabs-demo.html — ARIA tablist, keyboard nav

Toast (inline, since it's the simplest one)

Hover a toast to pause its timer. Esc does nothing here, click the ×.
